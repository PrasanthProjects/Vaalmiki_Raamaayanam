**Source note:** The text explicitly names Kaikeyi's tone here as "తేలికగా" (lightly/casually) — this is not a mother struggling to break devastating news gently, but someone announcing it almost offhandedly; a deliberately chilling character beat, worth playing exactly as written rather than softened into more sympathetic delivery.

**Source note:** The "maddened elephant" simile is a genuinely violent, physical image for grief — a large, uncontrolled, thrashing collapse, not quiet crying; render with real physical intensity, matching the severity the text's own simile demands.
