**Source note:** Bharata's reasoning is entirely, explicitly about preserving his future relationship with Rama, not about mercy toward either Kaikeyi or Manthara — a consistent, sharp-edged version of the same "what will Rama think" logic that has governed his own conduct since learning the truth; worth playing without any softening of his continued anger toward both women, since he states plainly he still wants them dead, only restraining himself for Rama's sake.
