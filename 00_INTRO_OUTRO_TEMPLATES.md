# పురాణ కథా శ్రవణం (Purana Katha Shravanam) — Channel Branding & Intro/Outro Templates

**Channel name:** పురాణ కథా శ్రవణం (Purana Katha Shravanam) — "listening to the stories of the Puranas"

This file defines the FIXED wrapper every video carries — the same bumper, every episode, every short, from E0.01 to the final episode of Yuddha Khaanda. Per the same consistency discipline as the Character Bible: don't redesign this per episode, don't paraphrase it — reuse it exactly.

**Where this fits in an episode:** `[SPOKEN TITLE BUMPER, ~4s] → [episode's own cold-open, from the episode's own script] → ... → [episode's own end-card teaser, from the episode's own script] → [OUTRO CARD]`. The title bumper and outro card below are new; the cold-opens and teasers are already written into each episode file (E0.01 onward) — this file doesn't replace those, it wraps around them.

---

## 1. Channel identity (for the logo/bumper's visual design)

- **Wordmark:** పురాణ కథా శ్రవణం in an elegant, classical Telugu typeface — a style consistent with the temple-inscription/Dravidian aesthetic already locked in `00_STYLE_GUIDE.md` (not a modern sans-serif logo font). Set it as real text in a design tool, never AI-generated — see `00_LOGO_BRIEF.md` for font choices.
- **Icon motif:** a kalasha (sacred pot) with a single drop of amrutam (nectar) falling from it, or a palm-leaf manuscript with a soft golden glow rising from its pages — either reads as "stories drawn from ancient scripture," fitting the name.
- **Color:** warm gold and deep maroon/red, matching the Ayodhya/Mithila palette from the Style Guide (this is a devotional-epic brand, not a Lanka-toned one) — this identity color should NOT shift Khaanda to Khaanda; it's the channel's own constant, separate from any in-story location palette.
- **Sound sting:** a single temple bell or conch (shankha) note, warm and brief — not a jingle with lyrics. Pairs with the visual in under 3 seconds.

---

## 2. LONG-FORM VIDEO — Opening title bumper (used on every episode, ~4 sec, SPOKEN)

**🔒 Fixed spoken line — use word for word, every single episode, same recorded/TTS clip reused every time:**
> పురాణ కథా శ్రవణం! మన పురాణాలు, మన ఇతిహాసాలు, మన జీవన విధానాలు!
>
> *(Purana Katha Shravanam! Our Puranas, our Itihasas, our way of life!)*

**Visual:** The పురాణ కథా శ్రవణం wordmark forming from a softly glowing manuscript page, kalasha/amrutam-drop icon animating beside it, warm gold light, temple-bell sound sting under the spoken line. Duration target: **~4 seconds** — tight and punchy, a title card, not a scene. Cuts directly into the episode's own cold-open (already scripted per-episode, scene 1 of that episode's own table).

**On-screen text (synced to the spoken line):** పురాణ కథా శ్రవణం

**Voice:** This line is spoken by the same Narrator voice/TTS preset locked in `00_CHARACTER_BIBLE.md`'s "LOCKED TTS SPEC" — it is literally the same audio clip reused on every video, not re-recorded per episode. Render it once, save the file, and splice it onto the front of every episode's export.

**Why this is short and fixed rather than folded into the episode's own cold-open:** a ~4 second spoken title card reads as a channel ident (like a production logo sting with a voice), not as narration content — it doesn't compete with or delay the episode's own cold-open hook, which still lands within the first ~10-15 seconds after this bumper. Keeping the line IDENTICAL every time (not "a channel intro paraphrased per episode") is what makes it recognizable — viewers should be able to identify the channel from this line alone within a second or two, the same way a TV network's ident sound is always exactly the same.

---

## 3. LONG-FORM VIDEO — Closing card (used on every episode, ~12-15 sec)

**Visual:** The episode's own end-card teaser (already scripted per-episode, e.g. E1.14's "తర్వాత చూద్దాం — జనక మహారాజు కొలువులో శివధనుస్సు కథ...") plays first, exactly as written in that episode's file. Immediately after it, cut to a fixed outro card: the పురాణ కథా శ్రవణం wordmark again (same treatment as the opening bumper), with a subscribe-bell icon animating beside it.

**Narration (spoken, fixed text — use every episode, word for word):**
> ఈ కథను ఇష్టపడితే, ఛానల్‌ని సబ్‌స్క్రైబ్ చేసుకోండి, బెల్ ఐకాన్ నొక్కండి — తర్వాతి భాగం కోసం. పురాణ కథా శ్రవణం.

**Notes:** This fixed line comes AFTER the episode's own teaser, never replacing it — the teaser is what makes someone click the next video; this line is what makes them subscribe so they don't have to remember to. Keep both, in that order, every time.

---

## 4. SHORTS — Opening (used on every short, ~1-2 sec, no narration)

**Visual:** A minimal version of the bumper — just the wordmark (పురాణ కథా శ్రవణం) flashing briefly in the corner or center, no kalasha animation, no bell sound (shorts autoplay with sound often off; a visual watermark matters more than an audio sting here). Cuts immediately into the short's own hook (already scripted per-short, e.g. S1.02.01's "ప్రపంచపు మొదటి శ్లోకం ఎలా పుట్టింది").

**On-screen text (persistent small watermark, entire short):** పురాణ కథా శ్రవణం — a small, unobtrusive corner watermark for the short's full runtime, not just the opening — this is standard practice for short-form to protect against re-uploads and build brand recognition even if someone scrubs past the first second.

---

## 5. SHORTS — Closing (used on every short, ~3-4 sec)

**Visual:** The short's own end-card line (already scripted per-short) plays first, then a quick cut to: పురాణ కథా శ్రవణం wordmark + "పూర్తి కథ ఛానల్‌లో" (full story on the channel) as on-screen text.

**Narration (spoken, fixed text — use every short, word for word):**
> పూర్తి కథ కోసం పురాణ కథా శ్రవణం ఛానల్‌ని ఇప్పుడే సబ్‌స్క్రైబ్ చేయండి.

**Notes:** Shorts have almost no time budget — this closing line must be brief enough to land inside the last 3-4 seconds without cutting off. If a specific short's own end-card line already implies "watch the full video," this fixed line can be trimmed to just the on-screen text with no added narration — use judgment per short rather than forcing the spoken line into every single one if it would make the short run long.

## How this connects to the rest of the project

- Every episode file already has its own **cold-open** (first script row) and its own **end-card teaser** (last script row, before the "Shorts derived from this episode" section) — those stay exactly as written.

- Nothing in this file needs to change per-Khaanda or per-episode — that consistency is the entire point, the same way the Character Bible's locks never change except at named points.
