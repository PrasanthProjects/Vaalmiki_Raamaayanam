**Characters:** Hanuman (in flight); Sagara, the Ocean (calm blue-toned royal figure, per E4.07); Mainaka (a golden mountain rising from the sea, then a golden-hued man standing on his own peak); Indra; the devas; Surasa, mother of the nagas (first as a vast, terrible rakshasi rising from the sea, then benign); the rakshasi Simhika (a shadow-catching sea-demon).
