**Characters:** Hanuman (first his own size on Mount Lamba, then no bigger than a kitten); Lanka's guardian deity, called Lanka (Lankini — a mountain-sized, fierce rakshasi at the royal gate; single-episode character); rakshasas of the city in the streets and houses.
