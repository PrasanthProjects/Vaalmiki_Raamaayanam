*Source: pp. 335-338 (from "అలా వస్తున్న రామలక్ష్మణులను..." on p. 335 to "...తన నిజ స్వరూపానికి వచ్చేశాడు" on p. 338; p. 337 is an illustration page captioned "సుగ్రీవుని పలుకున ఋష్యమూక పర్వత శిఖరముల వద్ద రామలక్ష్మణులను దర్శించిన స్వామి హనుమ" — folded into the visuals). Status: ✅ Reconciled between OCR and corrected-extraction passes. Two Sanskrit shlokas (Hanuman calming Sugriva; Hanuman taking a mendicant's form) are flagged for the manual shloka-audit pass — the corrected extraction has an unreadable glyph in "సర్వైః", read from OCR. "శ్రీ మహా విష్ణువుగా" (garbled in the corrected extraction) is read from OCR. Printing slips normalised: "చూశార" → "చూశారా", "రహస్యంగ" → "రహస్యంగా", "కొదండాలు" → "కోదండాలు", "ఎద్దుల్లా ల్" → "ఎద్దుల్లా".*

**Characters:** Rama (RAMA-S4), Lakshmana. **First appearances, per the VANARA DESIGN BASELINE (Character Bible — reference-locked):** **Sugriva** (golden/tawny fur, athletic, anxious and watchful), **Hanuman** (orange/reddish fur, powerful, intelligent, calm), and Sugriva's three other ministers (unnamed here; each with his own distinct face, build and fur tone). These five are the five vanaras who caught Sita's jewels in E3.25 — reuse those faces.

**⚠️ Hanuman's two forms in this episode:** (1) his true vanara form among Sugriva's ministers; (2) a **mendicant (bhikshu) disguise** — a human ascetic's form in saffron robes, with the same intelligent eyes — in which he meets Rama. The source says he returns to his true form on seeing Rama (scenes 23-25).
