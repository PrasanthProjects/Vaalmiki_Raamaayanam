*Source: pp. 204-205 (p. 205 up to Kausalya's grief at the funeral offerings). Status: ✅ Reconciled between OCR and corrected-extraction passes. One passage of two Sanskrit shlokas (Rama telling Sita and Lakshmana of their father's death) is printed in both extractions in a broken transliteration ("సీతె మృఇతస్తె ష్వషురహ్ …"); it is **reconstructed here in standard form and flagged for extra scrutiny** in the manual shloka-audit pass. Printing slips normalised: "బయలదేరు" → "బయలుదేరు", "నిశబ్దంగా" → "నిశ్శబ్దంగా". The narrator's aside on the walking order is read from the corrected extraction (OCR drops "స్త్రీ").*

**Characters:** Rama (RAMA-S3, exile stage), Sita (SITA-S3), Lakshmana (exile stage), Bharata (ascetic stage), Shatrughna, Vashishtha, Kausalya, Sumitra, Kaikeyi (all per their locks; the queens in plain mourning dress).
