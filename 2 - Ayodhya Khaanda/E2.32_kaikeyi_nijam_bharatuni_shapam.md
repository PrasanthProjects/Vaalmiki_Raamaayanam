# E2.32 — కైకేయ నిజం బయటపెట్టడం & భరతుని శాపం (Kaikeyi Reveals the Truth; Bharata's Renunciation)

*Source: p. 181. Status: ✅ Reconciled between OCR and corrected-extraction passes. No verses in this stretch requiring the audit flag — this scene is narrated entirely in plain, direct speech in the source, deliberately unadorned by formal verse at its most intense moment.*

**Characters:** Bharata (S1/boyhood-equivalent stage), Kaikeyi (S2).

**⚠️ This is one of the most emotionally intense scenes in the entire series so far. Do not soften Bharata's language — the source is explicit and severe, and its severity is the point: a son's complete, formal repudiation of his own mother, in the same register as Dasharatha's own renunciation of Kaikeyi in E2.09.**
