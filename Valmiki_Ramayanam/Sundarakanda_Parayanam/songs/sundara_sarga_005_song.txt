STYLE: Sung Telugu devotional story song to Lord Hanuman with a male lead vocal singing from start to end, Carnatic keertana style in raga Arabhi (heroic, veera rasa), Adi tala, 96 bpm. A strong, warm, full-bodied male Carnatic vocalist with light gamakas. Three Sanskrit shlokas are recited slowly in traditional parayanam chanting over tanpura only, with clear Sanskrit pronunciation. Vocals loud and upfront, the singer sings every line of the lyrics clearly, every Telugu word easy to understand, a short pause at each line ending, a catchy pallavi "Jaya Jaya Hanuma" that a strong devotional chorus joins, charanams lead-only. Conch and temple bell at the start, then nadaswaram, thavil, mridangam, bansuri, veena and small cymbals, heroic and devotional, rising energy, about 5 minutes.
EXCLUDE: rap, EDM, western pop, autotune, female lead, English words, mumbled lyrics
