• hanumatā vānareṇa – the vanara Hanuman
• saṅgataḥ ha – met

Meaning:
"Shabari worshipped Rama, the son of Dasharatha, with great devotion. Then, on the shore of Lake Pampa, Rama met the vanara named Hanuman."
