# Story Shorts

Each episode has its own folder containing a reproducible build, scene plan, Telugu dialogue, source audio, and final exports. Target runtime: 1½–3 minutes, driven by natural narration timing.

- `S01_Parichayam/`: introduction to Ramayanam; ending previews **E1.01 — వాల్మీకి మహర్షి కథ**.

Use a clear opening, connected narrative, complete verses, gentle transitions, and a specific next-episode teaser. Keep the Telugu Puranamrutham logo visible at the top. Use photorealistic existing assets and restrained digital movement. These are local edits of still images, not AI-generated character motion.

Narration uses already saved Telugu speech. No voice-cloning job or new online speech request is needed for this episode. New words in future episodes require matching audio rather than reusing an unrelated recording.
