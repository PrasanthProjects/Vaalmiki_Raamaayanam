# E3.22 — సన్యాసి వేషంలో రావణుడు (Ravana in an Ascetic's Guise)

*Source: pp. 287-288. Status: ✅ Reconciled between OCR and corrected-extraction passes. One Sanskrit shloka (Sita on her and Rama's ages) is flagged for the manual shloka-audit pass. "క్రీడించినప్పుడు" (garbled in the corrected extraction) is read from OCR. Printing slips normalised: "యదేచ్ఛగా" kept as printed; "అని అని పలికింది" → "అని పలికింది"; "పరివ్రాజక" read from OCR.*

**Characters:** Ravana (**disguised as a parivrajaka — a wandering ascetic**: saffron robes, topknot, sacred thread, water-pot on the left shoulder, an unnatural radiance; then speaking as himself — his true form is not shown until E3.23), Sita (SITA-S3). The forest, the wind and the Godavari react to him.

**⚠️ Presentation note:** Ravana's flattery (scenes 6-10) is described by the source itself as the talk of a predator (scenes 11-12). The visuals show Sita barely listening, her eyes on the forest where Rama and Lakshmana went — never a lingering look at her. Ravana's disguise must be convincing enough that her hospitality is natural, with only faint wrong notes (the stilled trees, the red eyes).
