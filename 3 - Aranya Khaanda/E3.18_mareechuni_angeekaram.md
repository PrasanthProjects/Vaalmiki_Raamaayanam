# E3.18 — మారీచుని అంగీకారం (Maricha Gives In)

*Source: pp. 276-277. Status: ✅ Reconciled between OCR and corrected-extraction passes. One Sanskrit shloka (the harsh king and his people) is flagged for the manual shloka-audit pass. The word before "త్రోవలో" is unreadable in the corrected extraction and missing in OCR; it is read as "తప్పు" from context — **flag for the audit pass**. Printing slips normalised: "అడిగార" → "అడిగారా", "ప్రోత్సహించి" read from OCR (garbled in the corrected extraction).*

**Characters:** Maricha (aged ascetic form), Ravana (per the E3.14 lock; two arms, as in E3.16-E3.17). In Maricha's memory only: Maricha's great-beast form and his two companions (monstrous deer-shaped rakshasas, stylised), Rama (**RAMA-S3**, as at the start of the forest years), Sita (SITA-S3), Lakshmana.
