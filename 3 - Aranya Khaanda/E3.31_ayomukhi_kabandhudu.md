**Characters:** Rama (RAMA-S4), Lakshmana (exile stage), Ayomukhi (per her Visual Lock — grotesque, sagging belly, no disguise phase), Kabandha (**first appearance — per his Visual Lock**: no head, no legs, a torso from chest to waist, one enormous eye and a huge mouth on the belly, two arms a yojana long).

**⚠️ Presentation note:** Ayomukhi's punishment (scene 3) is narrated as printed, but never shown: cut on Lakshmana's drawn sword to her flight into the cave, hands over her face. Kabandha's arms being cut (scene 6) is shown as two sword-strokes and the giant arms falling slack, no wounds.
