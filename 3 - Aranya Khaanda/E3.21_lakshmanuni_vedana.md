# E3.21 — లక్ష్మణుని వేదన (Lakshmana's Anguish)

*Source: pp. 285-286. Status: ✅ Reconciled between OCR and corrected-extraction passes. One Sanskrit shloka (Lakshmana on the false cry) is flagged for the manual shloka-audit pass — printed "న సస్ తస్య" in both extractions; kept as printed. Printing slips normalised for reading: "కౄరాత్ముడా" → "క్రూరాత్ముడా", "ద్రువపరుచుకోడానికి" → "ధ్రువపరుచుకోడానికి", "కదులుతావ" → "కదులుతావా", "యదార్ధం" → "యథార్థం".*

**Characters:** Lakshmana (exile stage), Sita (SITA-S3). Background: the forest itself (the vana-devatas Lakshmana calls on — shown only as the stillness of the trees, no figures).

**⚠️ Presentation note (continued from E3.20):** Sita speaks in terror, not in her true character; keep her grief-stricken, not cruel. Her threat to end her life (scenes 5 and 8) is spoken, never visualised — no poison, rope or fire in any shot. Lakshmana never raises his voice.
