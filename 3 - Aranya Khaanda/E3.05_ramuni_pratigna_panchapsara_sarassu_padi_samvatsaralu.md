*Source: pp. 233-238 (from the last line of p. 233; pp. 236-238 are illustration pages captioned "ముని ఆశ్రమములను రక్షించుటకై రాక్షస సంహారము చేస్తున్న రామలక్ష్మణులు" and "అరణ్యవాస సమయములో 10 సంవత్సరముల పాటు ముని ఆశ్రమములలో కాలము గడుపుతున్న సీతారామలక్ష్మణులు" — folded into the ten-year montage, no body text). Status: ✅ Reconciled between OCR and corrected-extraction passes. Two Sanskrit shlokas (Rama on the sages' refuge; Dharmabhrut on Mandakarni's lake) are flagged for the manual shloka-audit pass. Printing slips normalised for reading: "వసుడయ్యాడు" → "వశుడయ్యాడు"; the corrected extraction's garbled "విహడిసూత్" is read from OCR as "క్రీడిస్తూ".*

**Characters:** Rama (RAMA-S3 → **RAMA-S4 from scenes 23-25 onward**), Sita (SITA-S3), Lakshmana (exile stage, still clean-shaven), the sage Dharmabhrut (one-scene figure, generic elderly-sage template), Sage Sutikshna.

**⚠️ Stage change in this episode:** the ten-year montage (scenes 23-25) is the natural point to move Rama from RAMA-S3 to RAMA-S4 (full jataa, visible beard, sun-weathered skin), per the Character Bible's "S4 — rest of Aranya" rule. Every Rama prompt from scenes 23-25 onward, and in all later Aranya episodes, uses RAMA-S4. Sita stays SITA-S3 (valkala, single plain braid) until the abduction; Lakshmana ages ten years but stays clean-shaven.
