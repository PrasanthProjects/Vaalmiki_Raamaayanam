*Source: pp. 273-275 (p. 273 and p. 275 in full; p. 274 is an illustration page captioned "సీతాపహరణము వద్దని రావణుడికి చెప్తున్న మారీచుడు" — folded into the visuals). Status: ✅ Reconciled between OCR and corrected-extraction passes. Four Sanskrit shlokas are flagged for the manual shloka-audit pass (on counsel that is bitter but wholesome; "రామో విగ్రహవాన్ ధర్మః"; on not displeasing Rama; young Rama at Vishwamitra's sacrifice). Unreadable glyphs in the corrected extraction ("శ్రోతా", "శ్రీమాన్") are read from OCR. Printing slip normalised: "కామించన" → "కామించిన".*
