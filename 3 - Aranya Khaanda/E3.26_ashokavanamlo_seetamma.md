# E3.26 — అశోకవనంలో సీతమ్మ (Sita in the Ashoka Grove)

*Source: pp. 300-302 (p. 302 up to "అశోక వనంలో శోకంతో ఏడుస్తూ ఆ సీతమ్మ ఉంది"). Status: ✅ Reconciled between OCR and corrected-extraction passes. Three Sanskrit shlokas (Sita's grief; Ravana's scorn for Rama; Ravana at Sita's feet) are flagged for the manual shloka-audit pass. Printing slips normalised: "విహడించడానికి" read from OCR as "క్రీడించడానికి", "అలాప్యురాద్ యం" → "అల్పాయుర్దాయం", "దిగ్భ్రాంతి" normalised.*

**Characters:** Sita (**SITA-S4, captivity** — from here: the same valkala, visibly worn, dishevelled hair, never in Lankan jewellery; seated under the Ashoka-tree motif per the Character Bible), Ravana (true form; arms simplified in this palace-conversation episode), rakshasi guards (generic grim-faced rakshasis — Trijata and the named rakshasis are introduced in Sundara Khaanda, not here).

**Location register:** Lanka's inner palace (gold, diamond-latticed windows, step-wells, the Pushpaka vimana) and then the **Ashoka grove** — fix its look here for Sundara Khaanda: a lush, beautiful garden that feels like a prison.
