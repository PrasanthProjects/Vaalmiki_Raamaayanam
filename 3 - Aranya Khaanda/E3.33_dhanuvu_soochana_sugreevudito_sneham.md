*Source: pp. 325-326 (p. 326 up to "...అని చెప్పి వెళ్ళిపోయాడు"). Status: ✅ Reconciled between OCR and corrected-extraction passes. No Sanskrit shlokas on these pages. **Characters:** Rama (RAMA-S4), Lakshmana, Dhanu (**radiant gandharva form, per Kabandha's Visual Lock — the post-cremation transformation**). Mentioned only (no full appearance yet — their introductions belong to Kishkindha Khaanda): Sugriva and Vali. Shown briefly in Dhanu's description: Pampa lake, Rishyamuka hill, its elephants, the unfading garlands, and a distant vanara silhouette on a rock.
